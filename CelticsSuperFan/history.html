Retired Jerseys in the Rafters

    Center (1980–1994)
    3× NBA Champion, 9× All-Star, Celtics' all-time leader in games played and rebounds.
  • 1 - Walter A. Brown
    Founder & Owner (1946–1964)
    Founded the Celtics; instrumental in NBA's formation and the All-Star Game's creation.
  • 2 - Red Auerbach
    Coach & Executive (1950–2006)
    9× NBA Champion as coach; drafted NBA's first Black player; first Black head coach.

    Guard (1983–1990)
    3× NBA Champion (2 with Celtics), 5× All-Star, known for clutch defense and leadership.

    Forward (2007–2013)
